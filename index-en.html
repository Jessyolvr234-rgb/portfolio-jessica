01 — ABOUT ME

Beyond
the code.

I'm a Systems Analysis and Development student passionate about turning ideas into digital solutions.

I'm studying Systems Analysis and Development at Unipê, with an interest in software development, web systems, and building digital solutions.

My journey in technology is driven by curiosity about how applications work, from the interface people use to the logic that processes information and the structure that organizes data.

I'm currently improving my skills in programming, web development, databases, systems modeling, and interface prototyping. Through academic and personal projects, I put what I learn into practice and explore each stage of software development.

I enjoy organizing ideas, planning solutions, and exploring different ways to turn requirements into functional projects. I also value continuous learning, creativity, and attention to detail.

My goal is to earn a software development internship, especially in Full Stack, where I can contribute to real projects, learn from experienced professionals, and keep growing as a developer.
